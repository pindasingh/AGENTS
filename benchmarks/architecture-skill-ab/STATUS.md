# Status and known blockers

## Reusable design

Complete:

- non-invokable location and identity;
- generic A/B candidate contract for one skill or a skill chain;
- configuration, per-run score, and blind-judge evidence schemas with fail-closed preflight validation;
- externally preservable preparation/review commitments, hashed input and blinded-submission manifests, sealed random mapping, HMAC-reconstructed judge order, deterministic run plans, required run evidence, and pair-aware aggregation;
- architecture-specific rubric, gold-fact contract, blind protocol, runbook, and completion checklist;
- calibration/confirmatory separation and confidence/error-aware winner rule;
- preserved eShop pilot as an example instance rather than a built-in candidate pair.

## Preserved pilot

Complete as directional historical evidence:

- four executor outputs: two candidates × broad/sequence requests;
- authoritative sources, final artifacts, native/static validation, and offline browser evidence;
- two reversed-order model comparisons per case;
- raw reviewer scores, arithmetic corrections, and disputed-evidence notes.

The pilot cannot establish a general winner because it has one generation per candidate/case, only two model judges, uncontrolled completion budgets, incomplete timing/token capture, a superseded rubric, and an eShop fixture already represented in candidate instructions.

## Blocks before a definitive benchmark

1. **Confirmatory evidence does not yet exist.** Five unseen/renamed neutral evidence packs plus the separate format-fit case need immutable snapshots and two-architect adjudicated atomic gold sheets.
2. **No compatible Mermaid renderer was installed for the historical pilot.** Its Mermaid source is authoritative, but the Show Me HTML is a summary rather than a rendered Mermaid diagram. A future configuration must pin an already-available renderer or record this as candidate B's delivery limitation.
3. **Historical executor metrics are unrecoverable.** Subagent token/duration callbacks were not saved when the pilot ran; they must not be imputed. Future runs must capture them immediately.
4. **Required confirmatory judging has not occurred.** At least three judges, answer timing, prior format experience, and agreement statistics are still required.
5. **The 24-run public calibration and 36-run confirmatory campaign were designed but not executed.** Their cost and duration require an explicit future benchmark run.

None of these blocks prevents configuring and running a new A/B comparison. They prevent claiming that the preserved pilot is a complete general benchmark or definitive winner.
